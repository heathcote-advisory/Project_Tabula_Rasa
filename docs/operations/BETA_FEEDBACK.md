# Beta support and product learning

This procedure owns the minimum private support and qualitative learning loop.
[Support operations](SUPPORT.md) owns privileged diagnostics; [incident response](INCIDENT_RESPONSE.md)
owns containment; [customer policies](../legal/READINESS.md) owns disclosure review.
The [launch EPIC](https://github.com/heathcote-advisory/Project_Tabula_Rasa/issues/224)
retains staged authority under the commercialization initiative. Internal synthetic
dry runs can proceed; unrelated users and real feedback require explicit beta
admission. No customer contact, invitation, tracking or paid activation follows
from implementation.

## Private contact and intake

`/help` is public and available without a business or working login. It uses
heathcoteadvisoryllc@gmail.com, the owner's selected contact. Mailbox selection is
not evidence of delivery or coverage; verify actual receipt/reply and restricted
access before admission. The application creates no support ticket or automatic
email. Users select a category, optionally enter bounded safe request/reproduction
and release references, review a preview, then explicitly send through their mail
client. Users without a configured mail client can copy the preview into email.
No session, tenant, current URL, logs, screenshots or receipts are attached.

The consent notice identifies email processing and its sender-address exposure.
Qualitative research is separately unchecked and optional; help remains usable
without it. Unchecking research clears its answers. No analytics SDK, usage
beacon, background SQL analysis or marketing enrollment is introduced. Necessary
security telemetry continues under its own operational purpose, independently
of research participation. Browser help drafts are memory-only and disappear on
navigation; do not persist them in storage, telemetry or error messages.

Support is best-effort with an ordinary first-response target within two business
days, excluding weekends and holidays observed by the operator, with weekly
triage. No repair deadline or 24/7 staffed coverage is promised. Before admission,
record the operator's time zone/holiday calendar and coverage privately and
communicate it with cohort expectations. Statutory privacy clocks and P0
containment on detection are independent of this ordinary response target.

## Classification and accountable handoff

The service owner is primary operator and initial defect owner; no support team
or additional privileges are implied. Keep the received time/time zone, private
case reference, consent choice and scope, provisional/final severity, safe
reproduction/release, assigned person, next review and response in restricted case
custody. Verify actual sharing and retention with the privacy reviewer. Preserve
original privacy-request receipt dates and apply case-specific legal deadlines.
Do not promise automated deletion: no case purge is enabled, and holds and
provider copies require the lifecycle procedure. Minimize copied data and review
research withdrawal/retention at each cohort review; stop collecting optional
research by omitting the questionnaire and communicate any separately required
support/security preservation.

| Verified severity | Trigger and disposition | Owner and review |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P0 | Suspected/confirmed cross-business breach, serious security incident, loss/corruption. Contain/escalate **on detection**, preserve evidence and follow lawful notification. Stop unsafe expansion/operations. | Service owner immediately invokes incident procedure; a support email target is not continuous detection or alert-delivery proof. |
| P1 | Material core-bookkeeping, export, receipt or access outage. Prioritize before routine requests; unaddressed material risk blocks admission/expansion. | Service owner assigns a named defect owner and next investigation checkpoint promptly. |
| P2 | Noncritical defect or privacy-intake routing. A privacy case can have separate mandatory urgency and is never deferred past its legal deadline. | Named owner, workaround/disclosure and explicit next weekly review; escalate material risk. |
| P3 | Suggestion or optional product-learning signal. | Product owner, documented decision and review point. |

The Help category supplies a conservative provisional priority, not a conclusive
severity assessment. Confirm impact rather than treating every bookkeeping
question as an outage. Merge duplicates while preserving distinct safe evidence.
For P2/P3 deferral record owner, workaround/customer disclosure, reason and review
point. Escalate unknown security/data-integrity impact rather than downgrading
to fit capacity. Before expanding from three businesses to ten/twenty, reassess
workload, unresolved material risks and operator capacity under a new release decision.

Use safe request/release references with existing least-privilege support tools
and aggregate reliability signals. Do not query arbitrary ledger rows or ask for
tokens to diagnose a case. The diagnostic capability grants remain independent
of business roles. No direct SQL is needed for this intake workflow.

For an actionable defect, manually prepare a sanitized roadmap issue containing
only generic task, expected/actual behavior, synthetic reproduction, release,
severity, assigned owner and validation plan. Remove sender/customer names,
business/tenant IDs, case/request references, financial contents, attachments and
private links. Obtain authorized GitHub-write approval before creating it; verify
the published content and link the issue **inside the private case**, not vice
versa. An issue is assigned only when an actual owner accepts it. Keep optional
research answers out of public issues even when pseudonymous.

## Measures and review template

Use opted-in qualitative interviews/manual counts, not new automatic tracking.
Include solo professionals and rental/side-business operators alongside owners
of multiple independent businesses in approved U.S./Canada cohorts. Keep business
count separate from distinct owner/user counts. Never infer retention from an
open session or represent three initial businesses as statistical validation.

At weekly triage and each cohort review, fill this record in restricted evidence
custody; place only sanitized conclusions in the owning release/product discussion:

- Review period, exact release/cohort admission authority, owner, sample size,
  countries/currencies and consent/withdrawal exclusions. Distinguish synthetic
  exercises from real observations; missing responses are unknown.
- Activation: count opted-in participants who completed an initial ledger task
  divided by those who attempted it; define the task and observation window.
- Task success: completed supported bookkeeping/receipt/export tasks over attempted
  tasks, with reported blockers and missing workflows. Separate provider failures,
  user confusion and unsupported tasks rather than inventing completion.
- Engagement/retention: participants voluntarily reporting repeat useful work in
  the next defined week over those eligible and responding. Report both response
  count and eligible count; do not equate silence with churn or retention.
- Satisfaction, perceived quality, confidence and switching barriers; independent
  scope value; actual need for second/third business and collaboration.
- Pricing research: preferred owner/account versus business versus collaborator
  basis; directional monthly amount and currency; value of additional-business
  capacity. No final price/provider/seat promise or agreement to pay.
- Support effort: case count by severity, first-response business-day timing,
  operator minutes and unresolved/recurring defects. Infrastructure/recovery,
  provider and administrative costs allocated per business and per user; explain
  shared-cost allocation and estimates separately from observed costs.
- Outcomes: assigned defects and issue links, accepted limitations/disclosures,
  next review, research withdrawal/retention decisions and expansion recommendation.
  Carry evidence limits and dissent. Paid product/pricing and paid release remain
  later explicit owner decisions, informed rather than authorized by this record.

## Internal dry run and release acceptance

Use synthetic cases for (1) receipt outage P1, (2) possible scope breach P0,
(3) routine defect P2, (4) suggestion P3, (5) privacy request with legal-clock
escalation, and (6) research declined/withdrawn. Assign a test owner, use synthetic
steps, draft a sanitized issue without posting, and record private-case to issue
linkage. Verify the public draft contains no case/tenant identifiers or payloads.
Exercise mail-client unavailable fallback and cancellation without transmission.
Local UI/transport/isolation tests establish code behavior; the operator still
must verify deployed form accessibility, mailbox receipt/reply, case custody,
assignment/escalation and weekly review before customer admission. Record actual
outcomes in the release discussion, never as a dated repository evidence bundle.
