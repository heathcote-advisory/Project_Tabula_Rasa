## Information and purposes

We process named identity, verified email, membership and session information to authenticate users and enforce separate business permissions. Business records include account and transaction details, payees, classifications, descriptions, amounts, journals, mileage, recurring records, document metadata and receipts. Authorized members can view information within their own business permissions; common ownership does not consolidate books or expose another business automatically.

We process records to deliver requested bookkeeping, document storage, exports, security, recovery and support. Necessary security processing is distinct from optional product research. We do not assume that use of the service supplies every legally required consent. The applicable legal basis and individual rights for each jurisdiction must be established in the reviewed release disclosures.

## Providers and locations

Render hosts the API/browser runtime. Neon PostgreSQL stores business records and control-plane identity/session data. ZITADEL supplies hosted sign-in. Google Drive stores customer-owned receipt files and connected-account information; encrypted Google OAuth grants and recovery bindings are held in operator-controlled credential custody, separately from ordinary browser responses. Synology NAS recovery custody retains independent database and receipt-byte copies where configured. Better Stack receives minimized operational telemetry. GitHub holds source/build and deployment workflow evidence, not customer financial attachments or private support cases. Email and restricted operator evidence custody handle support/privacy cases. Provider processing can occur outside your country; exact deployed regions, contracts, access and provider retention limitations require verification before external release, and no all-Canada or all-U.S. residency claim is made.

## Google information

Customer-initiated Google OAuth uses drive.file for app-created or explicitly selected files, not unrestricted Drive access. We use the grant for the selected business's receipt operations and recovery; authorized operators may use approved independent recovery custody. We do not sell Google information, use it for advertising, or use it to train generalized AI models. We do not ask you to paste Google passwords or tokens into feedback. Disconnect preserves files and ledger links; revoke the grant separately in Google if desired. Revocation may affect receipt access and recovery, so export needed files first.

Optional Maps requests send the needed location/request information to Google. Autocomplete and route suggestions are transient; no route polylines or persistent Place IDs are stored. Only user-reviewed saved location text and mileage become business records. Google's own processing follows its Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and Maps terms (https://maps.google.com/help/terms_maps/).

Essential same-origin cookies support sign-in transactions, authenticated sessions and CSRF protection. They are HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax and Secure on hosted HTTPS, scoped to the application host. The sign-in provider manages its own cookies. Session credentials are not stored in localStorage. The application uses browser storage for selected noncredential workflow recovery and cross-tab sign-out coordination; these are not advertising profiles. Review your shared-device practices and sign out when finished.

No advertising, marketing enrollment, analytics SDK or automatic product-usage tracking is enabled by the feedback feature. Necessary minimized request/security telemetry remains independent of optional research. Help prepares an email draft only after you review its contents; sending is your explicit action in your mail client. The mail service receives your sender address and message. Do not include business identifiers, bookkeeping contents, receipts, screenshots, credentials or tokens. A technical request reference and release number can support private diagnostics. Optional qualitative research answers are separately chosen and can be omitted without losing help. Contact us to withdraw research participation; necessary support/security/legal records may still require preservation.

## Retention, access and recovery

Authorized members can correct supported records; Owners can request complete bookkeeping/document handoff. Export delivery depends on current authorization and document availability. Business closure gives a 60-day read-only Owner export/recovery window; after that ordinary access ends, with data retained pending reviewed disposition. Login closure revokes local sessions but does not erase business history or the external identity-provider account. Privacy review is available through the private contact, including after loss of application access.

No automatic live-data purge or routine backup expiry is enabled. Existing NAS generations are retained indefinitely. Proposed future 90-day periods are not effective deletion deadlines. Logs, mail, grants, audit history, customer-held Drive files, frozen sources and recovery copies have separate limits and legal/hold obligations. Provider history retrieval limits do not prove physical deletion. We cannot promise immediate removal from every backup or provider system. Before executing erasure we verify authority, legal requirements, holds and recoverability and explain applicable limits privately. Applicable statutory response deadlines are assessed from original receipt; manual review does not suspend them.

We share information only for the authorized service, necessary providers, verified support, security/incident response, legally required disclosures or a lawfully reviewed business transfer. We do not sell personal information for advertising. Membership does not authorize unrestricted operator browsing or impersonation. No electronic service is perfectly secure; protect your login and report suspected exposure privately. Serious incidents follow independent containment and lawful notification procedures.
